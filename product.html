To ensure an optimal, clean burn for your Signature Spell candle:

  • Trim the wooden wick to approximately 1/4 inch before each lighting.
  • Allow the wax pool to reach the container edge during the first burn to prevent tunneling.
  • Avoid burning for more than 4 consecutive hours.
  • Keep the lit candle away from active drafts, children, and pets.
