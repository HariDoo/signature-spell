Our Story

Home  /  Our Story
Born in Bangalore & Portland

Crafted for slow, conscious living

Founded in 2023, Signature Spell was born out of a desire to create a clean-burning, high-end olfactory experience that brings nature inside. We felt e-commerce was flooded with synthetic paraffin candles housed in generic vessels, burning away without character.

We set out to formulate a soy-coconut wax blend that acts as an excellent scent thrower, paired with natural wood wicks that crackle with comforting hearth sounds, all housed in vessels that look like curated design objects on your shelf.

"A candle is not just a room deodorizer. It is a visual cue to breathe deeply, a golden focal point that changes the ambient rhythm of a space."

Our fragrances are custom blended in-house using premium essential oils and organic botanicals. We do not use phthalates, formaldehydes, or chemical colorants. The result is a clean, soot-free burn that leaves only a pure, lingering aura behind.

Our Commitments

The Three Pillars

🌱

Botanical Purity

We source raw, non-GMO soy and organic coconut waxes. Our botanical scent layers are derived from sustainable growers around the globe, ensuring toxic-free inhalation.

🏺

Vessel Longevity

Every apothecary glass, golden brass, and coconut cup is selected for reuse. When your candle burns down, wipe it clean and repurpose it as a planter, jewelry cup, or spice container.

👐

Artisanal Pour

We pour in small, micro-batches of 20 candles at a time in our scent lab. This allows us to inspect wick placement, wax temperature, and oils concentration for flawless burns.

Behind the Brand

Aria Vance

Founder & Lead Aromacologist

Prior to establishing Signature Spell, Aria spent a decade in the French perfumery industry studying the neurological impacts of environmental scents. Her studies confirmed that warm, woodsy aromas can drastically reduce physiological stress markers in domestic environments.

"Signature Spell is the combination of clean-burning organic chemistry and emotional scent therapy. We build aroma structures that welcome you home, grounding you in the present moment."
